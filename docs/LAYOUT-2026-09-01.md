## Width

| Page | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `/dashboard/transfers/[id]` (client) | no cap — fills `main` | unchanged |
| `/admin/property-transfers/[id]` | `max-w-4xl mx-auto` (896px) | no cap |
| `/partner/transfers/[id]` | `max-w-4xl mx-auto` (896px) | no cap |
| `/admin/matters/[id]` | `max-w-4xl mx-auto` (896px) | no cap |

The client portal never had the cap. That one class was the whole difference
Zewn was pointing at. The layout shells (`app/*/layout.tsx`) already supply
`p-4 md:p-6`, so nothing runs to the window edge.

Columns: `xl:grid-cols-[minmax(0,2.15fr)_minmax(0,1fr)]` — roughly 68 / 32. One
column below `xl`, where a split would only make two narrow ones.

## Where the matters used to be listed, and where they are now

A service line tracks at most ONE matter, and a transfer can legitimately carry
two matters of one service (a rates clearance re-run after a failed one). The
second sets `matters.transfer_id` and attaches to no line. That is what the
"Other matters" card existed to catch, and it is why the card could not simply be
deleted.

It is now shown **under the service line of its own service**, inside
`TransferServices`. One place instead of three, and nothing attached to a
transfer is invisible on it.

The two jobs the deleted cards did are both still reachable from the line:

- **create** — "Open as matter" (`/admin/matters/new?transfer=…&service=…`)
- **adopt an existing one** — "Track an existing matter", staff only
