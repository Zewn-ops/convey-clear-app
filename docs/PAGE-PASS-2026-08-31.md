## Why this earns the time

The recurring defect in this codebase is not broken logic. It is **surfaces
drifting apart**. Every one of these was found by reading two portals side by
side, not by a bug report:

| Found | What had drifted |
|---|---|
| 2026-08-28 | "My Matters" hidden on desktop, left standing on mobile |
| 2026-08-28 | Matters block became an exception list on admin, stayed a table on partner — **one commit apart, and it survived three days** |
| 2026-08-31 | Notification page existed for admin only, while the shared list linked to `${base}/notifications` for every portal |
| 2026-08-31 | Partner overview linked to `/partner/matters` while that tab was hidden from the nav |
| 2026-08-31 | Service line could point at a matter on a different transfer |

## The five questions

For each page, in order, and **write the answer down**:

1. **What is the one thing a reader came for?** Is it in the first screen, above
   any card that is merely present because it exists?
2. **What is here that nobody reads?** Cut or demote it.
3. **Does the equivalent page in the other two portals agree** — same order, same
   names, same affordances where the permission is the same? Divergence must be
   deliberate and written down.
4. **Does every empty state explain why it is empty** and offer the action that
   fills it? (PRODUCT.md principle 5.)
5. **Is anything shown that this viewer cannot act on?** That is the class
   `f05466b`, `070` and `fbe9daf` all were.
